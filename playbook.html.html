Draft a PK/PD summary from a curve

~15 min research ops

What you're doing

You're given a drug-concentration–time curve from a PK study. Your job: estimate half-life, compute AUC, and write a short summary for a study memo. This is standard work in clinical pharmacology and translational research.

Realistic mockup: PK curve from a study

Your workflow steps

  • Read C₀: peak concentration ~9.8 mg/L (from the curve).
  • Estimate half-life: the red dashed line shows t½ ≈ 3.5 h (time to drop to half of C₀).
  • Compute AUC: shaded area ≈ 4.8 mg·h/L — total exposure.
  • Write summary: "Single 500mg dose: C₀ ~9.8 mg/L, t½ ~3.5h, AUC ~4.8 mg·h/L. Consistent with once-daily dosing."

Trace a graph & choose a data structure

~15 min algorithms

What you're doing

You're analyzing a protein-interaction network. Your job: choose BFS vs DFS for the task, trace the traversal, and justify the complexity trade-off in a short design note.

Realistic mockup: protein-interaction network
BFS from A: B,C (dist 1) → D (dist 2) → E,F (dist 3). DFS from A: A→B→D→E→F→C.

Your workflow steps

  • Define the task: "find shortest paths from A" → BFS; "check connectivity/cycles" → DFS.
  • Choose the structure: adjacency list (sparse graph, O(V+E) memory).
  • Trace BFS: queue = [A] → visit B,C → visit D → visit E,F. Order: A,B,C,D,E,F.
  • Justify: BFS O(V+E) gives shortest paths in unweighted graphs — the right choice for interaction distance.

Tune a CDS alert

~15 min clinical systems

What you're doing

A CDS alert is firing too often and causing alert fatigue. Your job: review the alert, propose a tuning change, and document the rationale for the clinical governance committee.

Realistic mockup: CDS alert in EHR
EHR · Medication Order · MRN 88421
Drug interaction: Warfarin + NSAID
Patient is on warfarin (INR 2.4). New order for ibuprofen 400mg may increase bleeding risk. Consider acetaminophen or GI protection.
Change order Override with reason Dismiss
Alert fired 42 times today · 38 dismissed · 4 acted on

Your workflow steps

  • Measure alert burden: 42 fires/day, 38 dismissed (90% dismissal rate) — classic alert fatigue.
  • Identify tuning options: restrict to INR > 3.0, or only fire for high-dose NSAIDs, or suppress if GI protection already ordered.
  • Propose change: "Suppress alert when INR < 3.0 and GI protection is ordered."
  • Document rationale: expected 60% reduction in fires with no increase in adverse events (based on local data).
